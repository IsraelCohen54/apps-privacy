Privacy Policy — Air Pump Finder

Draft of 26 October 2026, not yet in effect · Applies to the Android and iOS app Air Pump Finder (io.github.israelcohen54.gasstations) · עברית

Air Pump Finder shows drivers which tire-inflation pumps work. To report a pump, you sign in with your phone number. While the app is open it uses your location to list nearby stations. When you report, your precise location is checked to confirm you are at the pumps, and it is not stored. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Air Pump Finder is made by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We decide how your data is used (we are the "controller"). Contact: IsraelCohen54@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Phone number

You sign in with your phone number. Google, through Firebase Authentication, sends you a code by SMS. We use the number to give each person one account. Other users never see it.

Google sign-in (optional)

You can also link your Google account, so that you sign in again with one tap instead of an SMS code. Your phone number is still required. Firebase Authentication then also keeps the email address, name and profile-picture link of that Google account. We use only the email, and only as described under "Repeated false reports" below; other users never see any of it. You can unlink Google at any time in the app (Settings → Account): Google sign-in stops, and your account keeps working with the phone number. The name and picture link are removed then. The email stays with your account until it is deleted, because it keeps the false-report lock honest. A Google sign-in that never gets a phone number is deleted after 30 days unused.

Display name

You choose a display name when you register. Everyone using the app, guests included, sees it next to your current reports.

If you are set up as a station owner or worker, we keep that role and the stations it applies to. It is shown as a label on your reports, and it stops you judging your own station or ones nearby. It is removed when your account is deleted.

Your reports and the location check

When you report a pump, we keep: the station, what you reported, the time, and what happened to the report (for example, confirmed or contradicted by the next driver). We also keep who confirmed or contradicted whom. This decides your standing and protects other drivers from false reports.

With each report we keep the result of the location check: how far you were from the station, how accurate the fix was, how old it was, and which kind of fix it was (satellite, Wi-Fi or cell). We do not keep your coordinates.

Please note: a report shows that you were at that station at that time. That is what a report means.

Precise location

  • On your phone. After you allow it, the app uses your location to sort nearby stations and to show the report card when you arrive. It only does this while the app is open. Nothing runs in the background.
  • Finding stations. To list nearby stations, the app asks our database for stations in the area around you. This request reveals the area, not your exact position. We do not save it.
  • When you report. The app sends your recent location fixes to our server. The server checks that you are within 150 metres of the pumps and keeps only the result described above. Your coordinates are then discarded.
  • Without signal. A report made without signal is kept on your phone, with its location fixes. It is sent only if it is under 10 minutes old, and only for the account that made it; otherwise it is discarded the next time the app is online with you signed in.
  • For a check against impossible travel, we keep the station's coordinates and the time of your last report, not yours. The next report replaces it. After 126 hours it is no longer used.

Standing and rank

From the votes on your reports we work out your standing: stars, confirmed reports, a level (Newcomer, Regular, Trusted, Expert, Master), simple badges, and whether you are locked out for repeated false reports. Stars and level are shown with your name on station cards. We also keep counts and times needed for the weekly limit of 3 reports.

Settings

Your settings (language, text size, units, light or dark, vibration) are kept on your phone only. The phone also remembers that you agreed to location use, so it does not ask again.

Notices

We keep in-app notices to you, for example when another driver confirms or contradicts your report.

Device tokens

The app does not use push notifications yet. If we add them, the app will ask your permission and store a device token with your account so we can send them. This policy will be updated first.

Proof that you saw this policy

When you tap "I agree" to this policy in the app, we record which version you agreed to and when.

Guests

If you continue as a guest, we create an anonymous account with no phone number and no name. Guests cannot report. "Exit guest mode" deletes the anonymous account. An unused anonymous account is deleted automatically after 30 days.

What we do not collect

No advertising IDs, no analytics, no crash reports, no contacts, no photos. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal bases

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation, or similar laws, apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to run your account, show your reports and name, apply the report limit, and apply your choices.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep station data accurate, to judge reports, and to prevent fraud and abuse (the impossible-travel check, standing, the lockout, and the phone-number and email hashes described below). You can object; see "Your rights".
  • Consent: for precise location. You give it in the app and in your phone's permission prompt. You can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings. Without it you can still browse, but you cannot report.
  • Legal obligation: to keep a record of your policy consent and of deletion requests.

Who processes your data

We use Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) to host the app's database, servers and sign-in. Google processes the data for us under its data-processing terms. Google also sends the SMS code and checks that requests come from the real app (Firebase App Check, using Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iOS). These services handle technical data such as your IP address and device type.

Everyone using the app, guests included, sees on your current reports: your display name, stars and level, a role label if you are a station owner or worker, whether the report is still unverified, when you made it, how precise the location check was, and an internal account ID that is not your phone number. Other registered users can also look up your public profile by that ID: your display name, stars and role label. We share nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

Our database and servers are in the United States. Google's sign-in service may process your phone number in other countries. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Israel, your data is transferred to the United States. Google protects these transfers with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, which are part of Google's data-processing terms.

Repeated false reports: what we keep after deletion

If several drivers contradict your reports, you are locked out of marking pumps as working for a while. Deleting your account, or removing your number from it, and registering again must not end that lock early.

So, only while your account is in this locked state, we keep one-way, keyed hashes of your phone number and of any verified email address linked to your account (through Google sign-in). They are deleted when the lock ends. If your account is deleted while it is locked, the hashes are kept with only: when the lock ends, and how many times this has happened. No name, no reports, no account ID.

  • If you register again with the same number or email, the lock carries over.
  • If an account with that number is again marked unreliable (by new contradicted reports) and deleted within the period, the next lock is longer. From the third time, an account registered with that number within the next 12 months stays locked until we review it.
  • The record is kept until the lock ends plus 12 months (from the third time: 12 months from the last deletion). Another deletion starts the 12 months again. Then it is deleted automatically.

We keep nothing like this for anyone else. You can ask us to review a lock by email.
