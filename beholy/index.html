Privacy Policy — Beholy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app Beholy (io.github.israelcohen54.beholy)

Beholy collects no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. What you write and mark stays on your phone: the released app is built without the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. The only exceptions are your phone's own backups, if you use them (see Backup), and the optional one-time purchase, which Google Play handles (see The garden's one-time unlock).

What the app keeps on your device

To work, Beholy stores the following in its own private storage on your phone. None of it leaves the device through the app.

  • Your practices — what you write, and the details you choose for each one (which days, an optional time, how hard it is, its area).
  • Your marks — each time you mark a practice (or undo a mark), with its date and time; and the short note you write when you make up a missed day.
  • Your settings — language, form of address, the app's look and its theme; whether the garden is on, and your bedtime.
  • The garden's nights — for each night it watched, whether the phone stayed locked until morning, or the time it was first opened.
  • The garden's purchase record, if you buy the unlock — the signed record Google Play gives the app (see below).
  • Which achievement medals you have already been shown, so the same one is not announced twice.
  • Your phone's time-zone changes, so a flight across time zones is not mistaken for a missed day.

Your light, levels, streaks, achievements, personal bests and the garden's tree are not stored as such; they are worked out on the phone from your marks and nights each time.

Shabbat and holiday times

The app calculates Shabbat and Yom Tov times on the device, from a calendar built into the app and the city you choose (or, when none is chosen, your phone's time zone: on Israel time, Jerusalem's times). The garden draws day and night from the same city or time zone. The app does not ask for, read or use your location.

The garden

If you turn on the garden, a tree grows on the nights you leave the phone alone. Every night, from the bedtime you choose and for eight hours, the app notes on the phone whether the phone is unlocked. To leave calls out, it checks only whether a call is in progress, from the phone's audio state; it does not read who is calling, phone numbers or call history. At bedtime the garden opens over the lock screen. Nothing about your nights leaves the phone.

Home-screen widget

If you add Beholy's widget to your home screen, it shows today's practices there (their titles, how many are kept, and your streak's day) and lets you mark them. Anyone who can see your home screen can see them. The widget reads its contents from the app's private storage on the phone, where the app also notes your phone's language for it.

What the app collects: nothing

There is no sign-in, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that gathers data. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. On Android this is enforced by the operating system: the released app is built without the internet permission, so it cannot open a network connection.

The garden's one-time unlock (Google Play)

The garden's first 40 watched nights are free. After them it can be unlocked with a one-time purchase ("support the developer"). The purchase is made entirely through Google Play, on the phone, by the Google Play Store app:

  • Google handles the payment. Your card, name, email address and billing details go to Google, under Google's privacy policy and the Google Play terms. The app never sees them.
  • What the app keeps: the purchase record Google Play signs and gives it (the product, the order number, the time of purchase and a purchase token), so that the unlock can be checked on the phone without a connection. It is kept in the app's private storage and never sent anywhere by the app.
  • Each time the app opens it asks Google Play, on the phone, whether the purchase is still owned, so that a refund is honoured and a new phone gets the unlock back.
  • What we can see: as the seller, we see the order records Google Play Console shows developers (such as order number, date, product, price and country). We use them only to handle refunds and for accounting, and cannot link them to anything in the app, which sends us nothing.

Backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's own settings, Android may include Beholy's data in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. Setting up a new phone directly from this one may copy it too. Those copies are made and kept by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to them. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it kept on the device. A Google backup made earlier stays until you delete it in your Google account. A purchase stays with your Google account (Google Play keeps its record), so reinstalling restores the unlock.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Any new permission (for example your location for Shabbat times) and any new way for data to leave the phone would be described here first.
