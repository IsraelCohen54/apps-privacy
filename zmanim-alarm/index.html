Privacy Policy — JZmanim

Effective 13 August 2026 · Last updated 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Android and iOS app JZmanim (io.github.israelcohen54.zmanimalarm)

Who this policy covers. This policy is for the app JZmanim, package name io.github.israelcohen54.zmanimalarm, published on Google Play by the developer hartk. The developer is Israel Cohen, an individual. The app was previously named Zmanim Alarm. Contact: IsraelCohen54@gmail.com.

JZmanim collects no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app does not connect to the internet at all.

What the app accesses on your device

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's GPS position. It is used for two things, both entirely on your device:

  • Calculating the daily halachic times (zmanim) for where you actually are.
  • Warning you when your saved location no longer matches where you are, so your times don't quietly become wrong.

You may instead pick a city from a list that ships inside the app, in which case the GPS is not used at all. Your location never leaves your device.

Compass

The prayer-direction compass reads your device's GPS and magnetic sensor. The bearing to Jerusalem is calculated on the device. A location you save for the compass is stored on the phone alone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Custom ringtones

If you choose one of your own audio files as an alarm sound, the app reads that file to play it. It is not copied, uploaded, or examined for anything else.

What the app collects: nothing

There are no user accounts, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs that gather data. No information is sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

On Android this is enforced at the operating-system level: the released app is built without the internet permission. Android will not permit it to open a network connection even if a future bug tried to. Everything the app needs is bundled inside it — the zmanim calculations, the timezone database, the list of cities, the geomagnetic model and the fonts.

Permissions, and why each one is requested

None of these transmit data. They are all local device functions.

Links to other websites

The app contains links to outside sites, such as yeshiva.org.il and ChaiTables, which explain the halachic sources the times follow. These open in your own browser, and only when you tap them. Once you leave the app, the privacy policy of that site applies, not this one.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Because the app is offline by design, any future feature that required a network connection would be a significant change and would be described here.

מה האפליקציה קוראת במכשיר

אם ניתנה הרשאה, האפליקציה קוראת את מיקום ה-GPS של המכשיר. הוא משמש לשני דברים, שניהם במכשיר עצמו בלבד: חישוב הזמנים ההלכתיים היומיים למקום שבו נמצאים, והתרעה כאשר המיקום השמור אינו תואם עוד למקום שבו נמצאים בפועל. אפשר גם לבחור יישוב מתוך רשימה המובנית באפליקציה, ואז ה-GPS אינו בשימוש כלל. המיקום אינו יוצא מהמכשיר.

מצפן כיוון התפילה פועל באותו אופן: הוא קורא רק את ה-GPS ואת חיישן המגנט, והכיוון לירושלים מחושב במכשיר עצמו. מיקום שנשמר עבור המצפן נשמר במכשיר בלבד ונמחק עם הסרת האפליקציה.
